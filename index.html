Support

Questions, bug reports and ideas:

ozkankazan41@gmail.com

When you report a bug, please include your device model and what happened just before it.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. Deleting the game also deletes your progress.

How do I use a gift code?

Open Mail on the main screen and enter the code in the code field.

Do I have to watch ads?

No. Ads are optional: you only see one when you choose to revive or to double your gold.

How do I change my privacy choices?

Settings > Privacy options, where it applies. Details are in the privacy policy.

Which languages are supported?

English, Turkish, Italian, Spanish, French, Dutch, Japanese and Russian.
